About this service

Global Weather Forecast and All World Calendars

Weather data — Open-Meteo

We call the Open-Meteo geocoding API to turn a city name into latitude and longitude, then request the Open-Meteo forecast API for current conditions and a 7-day daily forecast. WMO weather codes (0 = Clear sky … 99 = Thunderstorm with heavy hail) are translated into plain language.

Every calendar culture

From the Gregorian civil calendar to the Chinese lunisolar calendar, Hindu/Vedic panchang elements (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana), Punjabi Desi and Nanakshahi months, the Islamic Hijri and Hebrew calendars, Persian Solar Hijri, Julian, Coptic/Egyptian, Mayan cycle, Buddhist/Thai (BE), Japanese era, and Vietnamese lunisolar calendars.

Ethiopian 13-month calendar

Ethiopia follows a 13-month year: 12 months of exactly 30 days, plus Pagumē with 5 days (6 in leap years). It runs roughly 7–8 years behind the Gregorian calendar. Our converter compares both systems month by month.

Time zones of the world

Live clocks for every IANA time zone with UTC offsets and daylight-saving flags. The 33rd parallel north page shows local times for cities sitting on that latitude line, from Casablanca to Phoenix and Nagasaki.

How the Sun moves and why the sky changes

Earth spins on a 23.5° tilted axis, giving day and night, while orbiting the Sun once a year. From Earth, the Sun appears to travel along the ecliptic through the 12 zodiac signs (and the 13th constellation, Ophiuchus). Because Earth occupies a different place in its orbit each season, the night sky faces a different part of the galaxy — so the leading stars and visible constellations change through the year. Axial precession slowly shifts the pole stars over a ~26,000-year cycle.
